PYKA 0.1.7 for Windows adds a Cartola tab, general-chat notifications and the new app icon.

- Follow Carvoeiro Champions League: all nine friends, round and season scores, individual players, captain markers and match schedules. Updates follow match times and pause when the tab is hidden.
- General chat now shows an unread count and can play a short message sound. Control both in Settings → Notifications.
- The new icon appears on the app, installer and PYKA System Helper.
- Calls stay connected while switching between general chat, Cartola and Lounge. Android is available separately as a preview download.

Updates download automatically and install when you exit PYKA. You can also use the installer below. The release retains the existing unsigned Windows preview status. Actual speaker, microphone and screen-capture behavior still depends on your hardware.

SHA256SUMS.txt contains checksums for the installer and update assets.
